TERMS OF SERVICE

01 Acceptance & Scope

By requesting a quote, placing an order, sending a payment, receiving a product, downloading a digital file, requesting custom development, or otherwise engaging CLTX4 for a service or product, the customer acknowledges that they have had an opportunity to review these Terms & Conditions.

These terms apply to products, digital files, websites, software, firmware, electronics projects, development services, custom configurations, research-oriented tools, design work, and other goods or services supplied by CLTX4 unless a separate written agreement expressly replaces a specific provision.

02 Orders & Confirmation

An inquiry is not automatically an accepted order. An order becomes confirmed when CLTX4 communicates acceptance and, where applicable, the required initial payment has been received and recorded.

Customers are responsible for checking names, specifications, quantities, colors, features, delivery information, contact details, and other order information before confirming. Screenshots, previews and descriptions may be illustrative where clearly identified as such.

CLTX4 may decline or pause an order when information is incomplete, payment cannot be verified, a requested activity is prohibited, or fulfillment is not reasonably possible.

03 Pricing & Payments

Prices are stated in Philippine pesos unless otherwise specified. A quoted price may have a validity period when stated. Third-party fees, delivery charges, platform charges, taxes, duties, or other external costs may be separate when applicable.

Payment records should be retained by both parties. A screenshot or message claiming payment does not by itself constitute verification; payment status is based on the payment information reasonably available to CLTX4.

Customers should never send passwords, authentication codes, private keys, recovery phrases, or unnecessary sensitive information as proof of payment.

04 Partial Payments & Remaining Balances

Where a project permits installment or partial payment, the initial amount is credited toward the agreed order total. For example, a ₱5,000 order with a ₱2,500 initial payment has a recorded balance of ₱2,500.

A partial payment does not automatically mean the order is fully paid, transferred, completed, or released. Unless a different written arrangement exists, the remaining balance may be required before final delivery, release of production files, activation of certain features, or handover of the completed product.

Customers should request written confirmation if they need a different payment schedule.

05 Refund, Cancellation & No-Refund Policy

GENERAL NO-REFUND POLICY: Payments may be treated as non-refundable after an order has been confirmed, work has begun, resources have been committed, a customized product has been prepared, or a digital deliverable has been supplied, except where a refund is required by applicable law or a separate written agreement provides otherwise.

Cancellation requests should be made as early as possible. A cancellation does not automatically create a right to a refund. If work has already been performed or third-party costs have been incurred, the amount retained may reflect completed work or committed costs, subject to applicable law.

If a product arrives damaged, materially differs from the confirmed specification, or a technical problem covered by an applicable warranty is identified, the customer should contact CLTX4 promptly with evidence so the issue can be reviewed and an appropriate remedy can be determined.

06 Delivery & Completion

Estimated completion dates are estimates unless a fixed deadline is expressly agreed in writing. Delays can occur because of supplier availability, shipping, component shortages, platform outages, customer response times, revisions, or events outside reasonable control.

For digital products, delivery may occur through a download, repository, website, cloud file, email, or another agreed method. Customers are responsible for providing working contact information and checking delivery messages.

07 Digital Products & Downloads

Digital products may include source code, compiled files, configuration files, graphics, documentation, templates, websites, firmware, or other digital assets. Unless explicitly stated otherwise, purchasing a digital product does not automatically transfer ownership of the underlying intellectual property.

Customers should make a backup immediately after receiving digital files. CLTX4 is not responsible for loss caused solely by deletion, device failure, incompatible software, unauthorized modification, or failure to maintain backups.

08 Custom Work, Development & Revisions

Custom projects may involve requirements gathering, design, coding, testing, configuration, hardware assembly, documentation, and revisions. The scope is determined by the agreed description, quote, or project brief.

Requests outside the original scope may require additional time or cost. A revision is a change within the agreed scope; a completely new feature, redesign, platform migration, or substantially different requirement may be treated as new work.

Customers should provide clear feedback in a timely manner. Delayed feedback may move the expected completion date.

09 Hardware & Physical Products

Electronic and DIY products can require appropriate power supplies, wiring, ventilation, compatible components, firmware, and safe operating practices. Customers are responsible for using products according to their documented specifications.

Unauthorized modifications, incorrect voltage, reversed polarity, liquid exposure, physical damage, overheating caused by improper installation, or other customer-caused damage may fall outside warranty or support coverage.

10 Support, Warranty & Troubleshooting

Support may include reasonable troubleshooting for the purchased configuration. Support does not guarantee compatibility with every device, operating system, network, firmware version, accessory, or third-party service.

Any warranty period, if offered, should be stated on the specific product listing, invoice, receipt, or written agreement. A warranty does not necessarily cover accidental damage, unauthorized modification, misuse, consumable components, or third-party changes.

11 Acceptable Use

Products and services must not be used for unlawful activity, unauthorized access, credential theft, fraud, harassment, destructive attacks, privacy violations, or interference with systems that the customer does not own or have explicit permission to test.

Security-related features, networking tools, radio features, scanners, development boards, and educational materials are intended for lawful learning, research, administration, testing, and authorized environments.

12 Security & Responsible Use

Customers are responsible for obtaining authorization before testing any system, network, device, account, or radio environment. CLTX4 does not grant permission to attack third-party infrastructure merely because a tool or feature is supplied.

Customers should follow applicable laws, platform rules, network policies, and responsible-disclosure practices. Any misuse of a product is the responsibility of the person operating it.

13 Intellectual Property

Unless a written agreement states otherwise, CLTX4 retains rights in original branding, designs, templates, source code, graphics, documentation, workflows, and reusable components created or owned by CLTX4.

A customer may receive a license or usage right as part of a purchase. The exact scope may depend on the product or project. Customers must not represent CLTX4-owned assets as their own, remove required attribution where applicable, resell restricted source material, or redistribute files contrary to the granted license.

14 Privacy & Customer Data

Only information reasonably needed to process an order, communicate with a customer, provide support, or maintain transaction records should be requested. Customers should avoid sending unnecessary sensitive information.

Where third-party services are used for payments, hosting, messaging, shipping, analytics, or file delivery, those services may process information according to their own policies.

15 Liability & Limitations

To the extent permitted by applicable law, CLTX4 is not responsible for losses caused by misuse, unauthorized modification, unsupported configurations, third-party outages, customer-provided materials, or circumstances outside reasonable control.

Nothing in these terms is intended to remove rights or remedies that cannot legally be excluded. Any limitation of liability should be interpreted only to the extent permitted by applicable law.

16 Suspension & Termination

CLTX4 may suspend or terminate a service, project, account, or delivery process when there is suspected fraud, non-payment, abusive conduct, unlawful use, material breach of these terms, or another legitimate operational reason.

Termination does not automatically erase payment obligations for work already completed or costs already incurred, subject to applicable law and the specific agreement.

17 Changes to These Terms

CLTX4 may update these terms to reflect new products, services, operational procedures, legal requirements, or security practices. The version shown on this website should identify the applicable update date or version where practical.

For an existing order, the terms applicable at confirmation may continue to govern that order unless the parties expressly agree otherwise or a change is required by law.

18 Contact, Questions & Disputes

Questions about an order should be raised directly with CLTX4 using the contact channel provided with the order. Customers should include the receipt/order number and a concise description of the issue.

Both parties should first attempt to clarify and resolve ordinary transaction issues in good faith. Nothing here prevents a customer from using any consumer-protection or legal remedy available under applicable law.
